# Training the exploration policy

Generated by `./gradlew run --args="train"`. The cross-entropy method ran for 25 generations of 40 sampled policies, refitting its Gaussian to the best 8 each time. Every policy explored the same 300 training mazes: 50 of each of the six maze types, 15x15 cells, from seed 77, which the benchmark never uses.

A policy's cost is its mean first-run steps relative to the freespace rule on the same maze, so the freespace rule costs exactly 1 and every maze type counts equally. Results on the benchmark's own mazes, which training never saw, are in [summary.md](summary.md#learning-across-mazes).
