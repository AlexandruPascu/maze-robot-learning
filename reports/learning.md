# Learning curves

Generated by `./gradlew run --args="learn"`: 100 mazes of 15x15 cells for each maze type (seed 2022, the benchmark's first 100 mazes of that size). Every robot runs each maze 30 times and keeps what it learned between runs. Steps are means over the mazes.

"Shortest from run" is the run from which every remaining run was a shortest route, averaged over the mazes where that happened within 30 runs. "Converged" is the share of those mazes. "Total" is the mean number of steps over all 30 runs.
